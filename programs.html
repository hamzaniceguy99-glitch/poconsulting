Program 01 · Mileage you can verify

Used cars and light vehicles

The Car-Pass is the single most useful document in a Belgian used car sale: it records the mileage readings taken at inspections and services, so a clocked car is visible on paper. We hand it over as the law requires, disclose what we know of the vehicle's history, and state the guarantee in writing before you commit.

Program 02 · Fit checked first

Parts and accessories

Model years overlap and manufacturers change parts mid-production, which is why ordering by model alone produces returns. We order against the vehicle identification number so the part fits the car you actually have, and we tell you when a cheaper equivalent is genuinely equivalent.

Program 03 · You own the artwork

Printing and pre-press

Pre-press is where print jobs are saved or lost: colour profiles, bleed, resolution and fonts. We prepare the file properly and send a proof, and once you approve it the approved proof governs — so read the names and numbers carefully. You must hold the rights to any artwork you send us.

Program 04 · Small runs welcome

Publishing and information services

Short-run work that larger printers decline: a catalogue of two hundred, a reprint of a manual, a set of documents that needs to look consistent. Files are kept so a reprint does not mean redoing the pre-press, which is usually the larger part of the cost.
